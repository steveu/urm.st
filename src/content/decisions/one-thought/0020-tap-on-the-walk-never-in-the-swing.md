A tap is punctuation between phases of a shot, never annotation within one. Its home is **clear**: the walk after the ball has stopped, out of execution, where nothing sacred is disturbed.

There the tap **records an observable** — a plain fact about what you just did: you aimed at the fat side, you ran your routine, you let it show. It never evaluates the shot or the decision. A fact survives being marked twenty seconds later; the result can't rewrite where you aimed or whether you took your breath. So marking at clear costs nothing in fidelity, and it protects the one thing that must not be touched — the swing, and the pre-shot routine that feeds it. That routine is the whole point of *Full Routine*; a tap welded into it would break one of our own thoughts.

The tap is forbidden inside the swing.

**The one exception is *Commit to the Line*.** Its content — *was I committed?* — is a felt state, not an observable, and hindsight rewrites it: "it worked, so I must have been sure." It can't be recovered on the walk. So it alone presses at **commit**, and it is safe there because its press *is* the commitment gesture, not admin bolted onto the routine.

The test that sorts the exception from the rule: does the tap record an observable (mark at clear), or evaluate a felt state (mark at commit, and only where the tap can be the act itself)? Almost everything is an observable, so almost everything marks at clear.

## Consequences

- **Timing is a separate axis from direction.** A ritual can mark at commit (*Commit to the Line*) or at clear (*One Breath*, *Ancient History*); a win or a catch marks at clear.
- ***Pins Are a Psyop* moved.** Its rule had said to tap at address, before the ball flies. It now marks at clear — where you aimed is a fact the result can't rewrite, so it keeps its protection against the outcome.
- **The count under-records, and that's accepted.** You will sometimes forget on the walk. It's the same under-count the catch thoughts already accept, and a small price for never reaching into the swing.
- **Every thought's dossier says when to press**, beside its on-course advice.
- **The first real round showed the exception working.** Carrying *Commit to the Line* over nine holes, I noticed the shots where I hadn't pressed — and the presses where I hadn't actually committed. A press without commitment is a tap that lies, and the mechanism made the lie visible to the one person who could know.

## Amendment — behind the ball, not at address (19 September 2026)

The exception originally said *Commit to the Line* presses "at address". Address is too late: the routine has started, both hands are on the club, and behind the ball is where I have always pressed. It now presses **behind the ball, as the line is picked, before stepping in**. No thought presses over the ball. A press falls either behind the ball, for this thought only, or on the walk.
