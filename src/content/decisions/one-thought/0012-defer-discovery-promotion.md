The map-work milestone bundled two things: the Moments resolution sheet — an optional free-text note on each moment you marked — and **promoting a discovery into a new thought**. In the glossary, a *discovery* is the bridge from moment to thought: always confirmed by the player, and the only sanctioned way words become a thought.

Promotion is an authoring cliff, not a checkbox. A thought is more than a name. It carries a family, a polarity (doing or quieting), a coaching line, a tap rule — and, since the rule for composing the round's sentence was ratified, an authored **read pack** of count phrases and clause settings that the sentence composer can't work without. Direction is authored, not inferred. And the gate that a count must interpret itself is a design review that had already removed one curated thought ([“Remove No Heroes from the thought menu”](./remove-no-heroes)). A player-typed thought would enter the menu with none of that examined.

The decision: **the resolution sheet ships note-only.** The promote option, and the whole discovery-to-thought path, are deferred to their own phase after the public line.

The language is unchanged — a discovery is still the one outcome a moment can have beyond being remembered, and free typing at choosing time is still forbidden. It just isn't built yet. Until then the library is the curated six ([“The menu seeds only documented thoughts”](./documented-thoughts-only)), and the read asks for feel, score, verdict and the note.

## Consequences

- [“The round-end Record: the full three-page read, map Moments included”](./record-full-three-page)'s full three-page read ships with its questions minus the promote option; this record is the honest note.
- No discovery is lost by waiting. The note keeps the words, and a moment is permanent, so a future promotion can reach back to any noted moment and carry true provenance.
- The deferred phase owes the design work the cliff exposed: how a player-authored thought earns a read pack and passes the self-interpretation gate.

## Amendment — 18 September 2026: the route, once it's built

The deferral stands. [“The conversation: tools for the player’s own AI”](./the-conversation-mcp-tools) records the intended shape for when it lifts. A discovery comes out of a conversation with the player's own AI rather than from a moment promoted on a form — no moment required; moments are a signal. It arrives as a proposal accepted in the app, its research cited only by reference to the checked research. The conversation does the examining this record said a form couldn't: should this thought exist, is it really one of the six again, can it be counted. The design work owed under Consequences is answered there, bar one item — storing count phrases as data.

## Considered and rejected

- **Full promotion now** — the player authors a name and a coaching line, and the rest is defaulted. Rejected: a generic read pack makes the composer mumble, and the gate goes unexamined at exactly the moment the library first grows.
- **Promotion-lite** — mark the moment as a discovery and defer the authoring. Rejected as a stored promise with no payoff: the note already keeps the words, so the flag adds state without meaning.
