A *thought* in One Thought is the single cue a golfer carries through a round — chosen from a curated menu, never typed in. The menu's starting set carried ten. Six had completed research dossiers: *One Breath*, *Commit to the Line*, *Pins Are a Psyop*, *Full Routine*, *The Heckler* and *Ancient History*. Four had never been through the research: *Swing at Eighty* (Process), *The Accountant* (Mind), and *Hold It Like a Bird* and *Finish in Balance* (Feel).

The dossier workflow had proven to be the proving ground, not paperwork. Of the seven thoughts researched so far, one was **removed** outright (*No Heroes*, [“Remove No Heroes from the thought menu”](./remove-no-heroes) — its count couldn't interpret itself); one had its tap rule **flipped** (*The Heckler*, from a ritual to catching a behavioural leak); and one was **rebuilt** around a different counting model entirely (*Ancient History*, which became a reinforcement count logged on every shot). Nearly half the researched menu needed surgery. The four unresearched thoughts had had none.

The decision: **a thought ships only with a documented dossier that ends in a decision to ship.** The menu is cut to the six documented thoughts. The four undocumented ones go to the bench, beside *The Career Eight-Iron*, until each earns its dossier through the research.

A dossier is necessary, not sufficient: *No Heroes* has a full dossier and stays out. And the research verdict isn't final either way — I can overturn it, as *Ancient History* shows. What can't be skipped is the reckoning itself.

## Why

- **Every count on the menu must have faced the gate.** A tap rule either interprets itself as a diagnostic ([“Remove No Heroes from the thought menu”](./remove-no-heroes)) or is ratified as a reinforcement count (*Ancient History*), and that judgement lives in a dossier and nowhere else. An unresearched thought is an unexamined count.
- **The record says unresearched thoughts are risky.** Three of seven needed flipping, rebuilding or pulling. There's no reason to believe the other four are cleaner, and some reason to believe they aren't, since nobody has looked.
- **The menu is a curated reading list.** Curation means the curator has read the book.

## Consequences

- The seed drops from ten to six, landing with the real-counting work. It has to land **before any TestFlight build**, so no outside tester ever carries an unresearched thought.
- The four families thin but all stay populated: Process has two (*Full Routine*, *Commit to the Line*), Mind two (*The Heckler*, *Ancient History*), Decisions one (*Pins Are a Psyop*) and Feel one (*One Breath*).
- The bench grows to five: *Swing at Eighty*, *The Accountant*, *Hold It Like a Bird*, *Finish in Balance* and *The Career Eight-Iron*. Any may return by earning its dossier; none is deleted from the record.
- The curated core now has exactly one way to grow: a dossier that ships. A player's own library still grows only through discovery.

## Considered and rejected

- **Keep the ten and backfill the dossiers.** Rejected: it ships four counts nobody has checked against the gate, and the backfill deadline would be "before TestFlight" anyway — the same work, with the risk pointing the wrong way.
- **Fast-track the four dossiers now.** Not taken as a blocker. The bench can be researched at leisure; the release doesn't wait on it.
- **Drop the four thoughts entirely.** Rejected: the coaching lines are good and the ground they cover — tempo, grip pressure, balance, card-counting — is real. They're unproven, not unsound.
