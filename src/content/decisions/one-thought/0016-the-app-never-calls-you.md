One Thought does one loop: **choose → carry → count → read**. You pick a single swing thought before the round, count it with a button in your pocket while the phone stays put, and read the round afterwards. Nothing else. No notifications, no dashboard, no feed.

The reason is the product's own thesis. A thought is meant to *graduate*: as the habit sets in, you need the reminder less, the count for that thought falls towards zero, and you drop it. The app coaches you to not need it. A streak is a dependency identity — it attaches to opening the app, and it can't survive the fade to zero that is the whole point. The mechanics that keep people opening apps would be features working against the method.

So the rule was set with the voice, before any of the app was built: the button is in your pocket; we'll be here after the round.

## Consequences

- **No notifications, at all.** The voice's own edge-state entry for them reads, in full: "Notifications: none. The app never calls you."
- **A refused vocabulary.** The voice bans *crush, unlock, streak, journey, level up, optimise, insights, performance, hack*. No badges, no confetti, no tips of the day, no onboarding carousel. No exclamation marks, no emoji, never all caps.
- **The read is read, not mined.** "Read your round", never "review your data". The after-round surface carries no charts, trends or per-hole drill-down — a drill-down rebuilds the dashboard the voice refuses.
- **Warmth is earned, not scheduled.** The caddie's notes arrive on real milestones, not on a timer, and every one must be written by a person before it ships.
- **Later decisions were routed through it, not around it.** When the growth patterns behind today's fastest-growing apps came up, the share layer kept them only where they could serve mastery rather than dependency — and its graduation card arrives like a letter, on a surface you visit, never a push ([“The share layer: postcards from the book”](./share-layer-postcards)). When AI came in, it came through the player's own assistant, not as anything running inside the app ([“The conversation: tools for the player’s own AI”](./the-conversation-mcp-tools)).
