The first real round, replayed into the app, put two moments on the wrong course. Proximity alone had snapped event after event from unmapped ground onto the nearest mapped hole, which belonged to a neighbouring course.

The fix was the **camping rule**. No hole holds a real round for longer than about 25 minutes, so a hole whose matched events span longer is being camped beside, not played. Every claim on it is withdrawn, taps and moments alike, and the read degrades honestly.

The rule was written down as **claim nothing rather than the wrong thing** — and it turned out to be the principle behind several decisions either side of it.

## Consequences

- **A zero or a whole needs every tap placed.** Any sentence saying "none …", "all …" or "both …" requires every tap to have been matched to a hole, because an unmatched tap could be anywhere. Proportional clauses count an unmatched tap against a pattern, never for it. Without this guard the sentence could state a zero the data couldn't back ([“The read is one sentence, and a sealed one never changes”](./the-read-is-one-sentence)).
- **A tap without a location is drawn broken, and still counts.** In the read's timeline, a tap the map never placed is drawn in the same brass, length and weight, but doesn't touch the line. Nothing is taken from the count; only the ground is missing. The voice line behind it: "No fix on this tap. The count still stands." The honest limit is written down too: the drawing shows whether a fix arrived, not whether it matched, so a well-fixed tap withdrawn by the camping rule still shows an unbroken tick.
- **No invented battery percentage.** The Flic reports volts only, and a coin cell's discharge curve is nothing like the straight line from 2.5 V to 3.0 V that a percentage implied. The figure and its glyph were removed. The one battery word the app speaks is the low-battery message at 2.65 V — the one that actually protects a round.
- **Words that don't overclaim.** The home page's door to the last placed moment reads "Marked · 14 August", not "last marked", because a newer unplaced moment may exist. Settings say "Paired." rather than claiming a live connection the app can't see. Presses made out of range arrive back-dated to when they happened.
- **Some notes become unreachable from history, and that's accepted.** On a course with a hole card, a moment that couldn't be placed has no honest surface in the history — its hole is unknown — so its note isn't editable from there. Accepted as the price of claiming nothing rather than the wrong thing; to revisit if the field misses it.
- **Some true things go unsaid.** Because holes played are inferred conservatively, a round that went quiet over its closing holes, with no moment marked there, will never say so.

## Considered and rejected

- **Trusting proximity.** Snapping every fix to the nearest hole looked right until a real round sat beside unmapped ground.
- **A percentage for the battery.** It looked more helpful and would have been made up.
