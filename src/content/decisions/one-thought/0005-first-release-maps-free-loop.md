The full Record with map Moments is in scope for the first version ([“The round-end Record: the full three-page read, map Moments included”](./record-full-three-page)), and that's a long road. I wanted something usable on real rounds well before then, and to put it in front of a few testers.

The decision: **the maps-free complete loop** — real counting on the Flic, plus the Cover and Sealed pages — is the **first release**, for my own pocket and a small TestFlight ring. **Map Moments is the public line.**

## Consequences

- TestFlight readiness — the App Store Connect record, signing, the location privacy label, a one-screen permission primer — moves forward into the maps-free milestone, not the map one.
- The loop — choose, carry, count, read — gets dogfooded on real rounds before the OpenStreetMap work lands, and that feedback informs it.
- The early milestones carry a higher reliability bar than a purely internal one would: a real round, in a pocket, must not lose data.

## Amendment — 21 August 2026: what the first release actually carries

The decision was written when the maps-free loop was months of work away. It was built in a day, and the train didn't stop there. By 6 July the app had the map Record, Course History and the Colophon (the settings page), and on 11 July it grew a second binary, the wrist ([“The wrist: a watch app as a second counter”](./the-wrist-second-counter)). Nothing was released in between. So the first upload isn't the maps-free loop: it's **the whole built app, phone and wrist, to a private ring**. (That was the position on 21 August. TestFlight uploads began in September, and by 19 September the app was on build 7.)

- **What ships in build 1:** the opening ceremony of choosing and carrying a thought, real Flic counting, the wrist as a second counter, the full three-page Record with map Moments, Course History and the Colophon — two binaries, one version pair, TestFlight, a handful of invited testers.
- **What "the public line" now means.** Map Moments was named the public line as a *feature* threshold — the Record wasn't honest enough to show a stranger without its holes. That threshold is met before the first upload, so the line stops being a feature gate and becomes a distribution one: the point at which the app leaves the private ring for the App Store. What it waits on now is evidence and housekeeping, not scope — rounds played by someone who isn't me, a ratification sitting, a copy pass, the App Store privacy label, and a ruling on the open Overpass proxy in the course service.
- **The dogfood consequence is discharged as overtaken.** "Dogfooded on real rounds before the OpenStreetMap work lands" can't now happen in that order: the map work landed on 4 July, hours before the first real round was played that afternoon. What it was protecting survived anyway — the pocket field test ran before any upload, and its findings did fold back into the map work (a replay on 6 July taught the matcher, among other things, that duplicate hole numbers mean loops). The remaining unmet half is a round on a mapped course, which belongs to the map work's own definition of done, not to this record.
- **The higher reliability bar stands**, and now covers two devices: a real round, in a pocket or on a wrist, must not lose data.
- **The gate from [“The menu seeds only documented thoughts”](./documented-thoughts-only) is met** — the menu seeds exactly the six documented thoughts, each with its research dossier.

## Considered and rejected

- **No release until map Moments.** Later real-world signal, and the heavy map work would ship untested by real rounds. Rejected.
- **Release at real counting alone.** Not a complete loop — it counts, but there's no read. Premature. Rejected.
